# Business Requirements Document — TerraWeave

**Project:** Sales & inventory reporting improvement (business-analysis case study)
**Note**
TerraWeave is a fictional company created for this project. The business scenario is inspired by Bangladesh's home decor and textile export industry. All data and business processes used in this case study are fictional.

---

## 1. Business background

TerraWeave is a fictional Dhaka based home decor and textile export business that sells handmade products, including cushion covers, jute rugs, kantha throws, wall hangings, basket sets, and table runners, to customers in the UK, US, Germany, Japan, Australia, and Canada.

Customer orders are received through email and social media. Sales, inventory, and shipment records are maintained manually across multiple spreadsheets and handwritten notes, making it difficult to track business performance and prepare timely reports.

## 2. Problem statement

Sales and inventory records are maintained across multiple spreadsheets and handwritten notes. Because the information is scattered and updated manually, the owner cannot quickly answer common business questions such as:

- How much revenue was generated this month, and how does it compare with previous months?
- Which products and export markets generate the highest revenue?
- Which products are slow-moving and occupying inventory?
- What is the current status of customer orders (Received, In Production, or Shipped)?
- Which products have reached or fallen below their reorder level?

Preparing monthly sales reports requires manually consolidating data from multiple sources, making the process time-consuming and increasing the risk of outdated or inconsistent information.

## 3. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in the project |
|---|---|
| Business owner | Requires a clear overview of sales performance to support business decisions. |
| Production manager | Needs visibility into product demand and inventory levels to plan production. |
| Sales representative | Tracks customer orders and provides timely updates to buyers. |

## 4. Business Needs

The proposed reporting solution should:

1. Provide KPIs for total revenue, monthly revenue, revenue by product, and revenue by export country.
2. Identify best-selling and slow-moving products to support inventory planning.
3. Track customer orders through each stage of the fulfillment process (Received, In Production, and Shipped).
4. Automatically highlight products that have reached or fallen below their reorder level.
5. Reduce the time required to prepare monthly sales reports by automating data consolidation and reporting.

## 5. Scope

The project includes sales reporting, KPI dashboard development, and inventory reorder tracking. Website integration, accounting, and a full inventory management system are not included.

## Expected Deliverables

- Cleaned sales dataset
- KPI dashboard
- Inventory tracker
- Business recommendations
