# TerraWeave — Business Analysis Case Study

A business analysis case study built around **TerraWeave**, a  Bangladeshi home decor and textile export company. The project demonstrates core business analyst activities, including requirements gathering, business process analysis, KPI reporting and market research.

Although TerraWeave is fictional, the business model is inspired by Bangladesh's home decor and textile industry, making the analysis representative of real world business scenarios.

## Project Overview

This project simulates how a business analyst would identify operational problems, analyze business data, document processes, and recommend improvements that support better decision-making.

### Project Deliverables

| Deliverable                      | Description                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Requirements Document**        | Documents business objectives, stakeholder needs, functional requirements, and expected outcomes.                               |
| **Business Process Analysis**    | Compares the current ("As-Is") workflow with an improved ("To-Be") workflow to identify inefficiencies and optimize operations. |
| **Sales & Inventory Dashboard**  | An interactive Excel dashboard for monitoring sales performance, inventory levels, and key business KPIs.                       |
| **Competitor & Market Analysis** | Reviews comparable businesses, identifies market trends, and highlights potential opportunities.                                |
| **Business Recommendations**     | Summarizes findings and provides practical recommendations based on the analysis.                                               |
| **Sales Dataset**                | A dataset used to perform the analysis and build the dashboard.                                                       |

## Dashboard Features

The Excel dashboard was created using a dataset containing export sales transactions.

Key metrics include:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value
* Total Units Sold

The dashboard also provides visualizations for:

* Monthly Revenue
* Revenue by Product Category
* Revenue by Export Country
* Order Status Distribution

An inventory tracker automatically identifies products that have reached their reorder level, making it easier to monitor stock availability.

---

## Business Problem

TerraWeave records customer orders manually across multiple spreadsheets, making it difficult to:

* Track inventory accurately
* Prepare sales reports efficiently
* Monitor business performance
* Detect stock shortages before they occur

This fragmented process results in delayed reporting and limited visibility into day-to-day operations.

---

## Proposed Solution

The redesigned workflow centralizes order management into a single structured system that automatically updates dashboards and inventory reports.

Key improvements include:

* Standardized order recording
* Automated KPI reporting
* Centralized sales data
* Inventory reorder monitoring
* Faster report generation
* Improved visibility for business decisions
