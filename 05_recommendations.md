# Business Recommendations

This section summarizes the key findings from the business analysis and provides recommendations based on the sales dashboard and competitor analysis.

## Key Findings

### Sales Dashboard

- Jute Area Rugs and Nakshi Kantha Throws generated the highest revenue.
- The UK and US were the largest export markets.
- Several orders remained in the "In Production" or "Pending" stage.
- Some products reached or fell below their reorder level.

### Competitor Analysis

- Large exporters dominate the market.
- Small businesses can compete through unique designs, flexible order sizes, and sustainable products.

## Recommendations

1. Increase inventory for high-performing products such as Jute Area Rugs and Nakshi Kantha Throws.

2. Review low-performing products and consider redesigning, bundling, or discontinuing them.

3. Focus marketing efforts on the UK and US, where demand is currently strongest.

4. Replace manual record-keeping with a shared spreadsheet connected to the dashboard to improve reporting efficiency.

5. Differentiate the business through contemporary designs and sustainable handmade products instead of competing on price.

## Next Steps

Implement the proposed reporting process for one month and evaluate its impact on reporting time, inventory management, and product availability. Update reorder levels based on the results.
